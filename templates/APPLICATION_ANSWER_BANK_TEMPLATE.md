## Voluntary self-identification

Add only the exact selections the candidate wants reused. Keep this private.

## Questions that always require review

- Legal attestations not already confirmed.
- Criminal-history questions.
- Security-clearance and export-control questions.
- Conflicts of interest.
- Government employment or lobbying questions.
- New salary terms that differ from the saved strategy.
- Any answer that would require inventing experience or credentials.
